# TV organization
A series owns seasons, and each locally available episode links to its existing playable item ID. The legacy movies table is the playable-item store internally; mediaType separates movies, episodes and unresolved TV files. Files, progress, sessions, audio and subtitle services continue to use stable playable IDs.
Recognize S01E02, 1x02, season-zero specials, combined E02E03 files and numbered files in Season folders. Default mixed roots auto-detect strong episode names. Explicit movie roots opt out; TV roots flag unknown names instead of treating them as movies.
Reclassification runs before the unchanged-file shortcut and once against existing registered paths at startup. It does not rename or delete source files. Normal reclassification keeps IDs; duplicates converge on a logical series/season/episode entry with progress merged by latest update and watchlists retained. Combined episodes remain a single playable file with an episode range.
TMDB TV search/details and season endpoints provide cached show, season and episode metadata. No unavailable provider episode is inserted as playable. Uncertain matches require administrator selection.
UI: TV Shows navigation, show cards/search, season selector, numerically sorted local episodes, per-episode resume/completion, continue watching, show watchlist, and next available episode. The common secure player is reused.
Acceptance: legacy imported episodes leave movie browsing, retain progress, group across seasons, rescan without duplicates, remain offline playable, and cannot bypass child-profile restrictions. Upgrade backups precede live database changes. Physical testing on the other device remains necessary.
